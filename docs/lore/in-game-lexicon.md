# The In-Game Lexicon: Web3 & Medieval Lore Glossary

> *"To speak of 'tokens' and 'contracts' in the presence of kings is the babble of mad artificers. But speak of 'Sovereign Deeds' etched in astral wax, of 'Alchemical Silver' refined in celestial crucibles, and of 'Royal Decrees' proclaimed from Citadel spires—and the realm will listen with reverence."*  
> — **Arch-Archivist Vaelen of the Citadel Scriptorium**

---

## Purpose & Immersion Philosophy

The **Sovereign Lands** universe bridges advanced decentralized mechanics (smart contracts, ERC-721 non-fungible tokens, automated market dynamics, DAO governance, and continuous 3D world spatial math) with high-medieval dark fantasy narrative.

To maintain 100% suspension of disbelief, modern technical jargon (e.g., *blockchain*, *NFT*, *gas fees*, *testnet ETH*, *minting*, *wallet address*) is strictly mapped to in-universe medieval, alchemical, and feudal terminology. 

This lexicon is the canonical reference for all narrative designers, NPC dialogue scripts, UI copywriters, and smart contract documentation.

## Detailed Thematic Lexicon

### 1. The Sacred Ledger & Arcane Cryptography

#### The Great Genesis Chronicle
- **Technical Term**: Blockchain / Distributed Ledger.
- **Lore Equivalent**: *The Great Genesis Chronicle* or *The Living Ledger*.
- **Lore Context**: An ethereal, indestructible metaphysical tapestry woven into the tectonic leylines of Aethelgard during the Cataclysm. Every action, deed grant, wall construction, and council vote is permanently etched into its fibers by the celestial scribes.
- **In-Game Dialogue**:  
  > *"No king, no fire, no passage of centuries can erase what is written upon the Chronicle."*

#### Living Covenant / Imperial Edict
- **Technical Term**: Smart Contract (`SovereignLands.sol`).
- **Lore Equivalent**: *The Living Covenant* or *The Sovereign Edict*.
- **Lore Context**: Ancient legal formulas inscribed with alchemical ink that possess autonomous life. Once sealed by the High Council, an Edict executes without mortal emotion, bribery, or delay—enforcing taxes, resolving sieges, and validating ownership automatically.
- **In-Game Dialogue**:  
  > *"Do not appeal to my mercy, Lord. The Living Covenant executes as the stars decree; I cannot stay its hand."*

#### Royal Offering / Etheric Tithe
- **Technical Term**: Gas Fee / Transaction Fee.
- **Lore Equivalent**: *Royal Offering* or *Etheric Tithe*.
- **Lore Context**: The alchemical catalyst required to awaken the Leylines and communicate with the Chronicle. Just as an envoy must offer silver to the bridge guards and burn incense before the high altar, a lord must expend a minute wisp of ether to register a decree.
- **In-Game Dialogue**:  
  > *"The Astral Scribes demand an Etheric Tithe before they will record your wall upon the realm’s roll."*

#### Signet Seal / Heraldic Cipher
- **Technical Term**: Public Key / Wallet Address (`0x...`).
- **Lore Equivalent**: *Signet Seal*, *Heraldic Cipher*, or *Lordly Mark*.
- **Lore Context**: A unique, unforgeable signet ring granted to every recognized noble who arrives upon the continent. When pressed into hot astral wax, it leaves an intricate cipher that identifies the lord across all courts.
- **In-Game Dialogue**:  
  > *"Affix your Signet Seal to this parchment, Lord, that the Citadel may know whose gold is pledged."*

#### Soul Signet / Master Arcana
- **Technical Term**: Private Key / Seed Phrase (Mnemonic).
- **Lore Equivalent**: *The Soul Signet* or *The Secret Word of Genesis*.
- **Lore Context**: The deeply guarded spiritual true name of a noble. If whispered to another, that person gains absolute command over all estates, vaults, and titles held by the lord.
- **In-Game Dialogue**:  
  > *"Guard your Soul Signet with your very life! He who surrenders the Secret Word surrenders his lands to the void."*

### 2. The Land, Deeds & Architecture

#### Sovereign Deed / Charter of Estate
- **Technical Term**: NFT / ERC-721 Token (`Parcel`).
- **Lore Equivalent**: *Sovereign Deed* or *Charter of Estate*.
- **Lore Context**: A cryptographic parchment infused with celestial starlight granting perpetual, unassailable ownership over a designated patch of continental soil. A deed cannot be revoked by royal fiat.
- **In-Game Dialogue**:  
  > *"This soil is mine by right of the Sovereign Deed granted under the 612 Covenant!"*

#### Cartographic Bounds / The Metes & Bounds
- **Technical Term**: Continuous 3D Spatial Bounding Box (`ParcelBoundingBox: minX, minZ, maxX, maxZ`).
- **Lore Equivalent**: *The Surveyed Metes and Bounds*.
- **Lore Context**: The precise spatial coordinates measured from the Keystone Bridge baseline. The boundary lines are physically enforced by harmonic resonance: attempts to build beyond one's metes cause masonry to dissolve into dust.
- **In-Game Dialogue**:  
  > *"Stay within your surveyed metes and bounds, neighbor. Your stone wall encroaches two paces into my barley!"*

#### Inscribing the Charter
- **Technical Term**: Minting / Claiming an Unclaimed Parcel.
- **Lore Equivalent**: *Inscribing the Charter* or *Staking the Boundary Stone*.
- **Lore Context**: The sacred ceremony where a pioneer drives a rune-carved iron stake into unclaimed wilderness and recites the ancient ritual of dominion, registering the plot upon the continental ledger.
- **In-Game Dialogue**:  
  > *"Drive the iron stake deep into the earth! The Astral Scribes have inscribed the charter—this frontier land is ours!"*

#### The Four Sovereign Structures
The tactical buildings that lords may construct upon their parcels:
1. **Farmstead** (*The Grangeworks*): Low timber-and-slate or stucco agricultural buildings that cultivate the soil, generating bounties of gold and grain every season.
2. **Guard Tower** (*The Sentry Turret*): Tall defensive bastions equipped with torsion ballistas and beacon braziers that project defensive power across neighboring parcels.
3. **Stone Wall** (*The Curtain Rampart*): Thick crenellated granite ramparts that form an unbroken bulwark, dramatically multiplying the kingdom's defense rating against foreign sieges.
4. **Castle Keep** (*The Citadel Bastion*): Colossal multi-tiered fortresses of state that serve as regional command posts, projecting immense aura, defense rating, and attack capability.

### 3. The Economy & Yield

#### Alchemical Silver / Aetheric Ore
- **Technical Term**: Testnet ETH (Sepolia ETH) / Continental Currency.
- **Lore Equivalent**: *Alchemical Silver*, *Aetheric Ore*, or *Crown Gold*.
- **Lore Context**: The universal medium of commerce and crafting across both Boreal and Solara. Refined from crystalline meteor fragments washed down the Great Divide river.
- **In-Game Dialogue**:  
  > *"Zara takes only pure Alchemical Silver for her silks. Keep your base copper in your pocket!"*

#### Reaping the Bounty / Tithing the Domain
- **Technical Term**: Harvesting / Staking Claim (`harvestYield()`).
- **Lore Equivalent**: *Reaping the Bounty* or *Collecting the Harvest Tithe*.
- **Lore Context**: The seasonal gathering of agricultural produce, mineral ore, and trade revenues generated by an active parcel. A parcel that remains untended yields no bounty.
- **In-Game Dialogue**:  
  > *"The sun has reached the autumn solstice; it is time to reap the bounty from the southern farmsteads."*

#### The Kingdom Treasury
- **Technical Term**: Smart Contract Treasury Vault (`treasuryGold`).
- **Lore Equivalent**: *The Royal Treasury Vaults* or *The Common Scriptorium Chest*.
- **Lore Context**: The centralized gold reserves held in the deep vaults of Frostfang Citadel and Solara Keep. Funded by a mandatory 10% tax on all parcel harvests, the treasury pays for kingdom infrastructure and acts as the grand prize contested in wars.
- **In-Game Dialogue**:  
  > *"The Citadel treasury swells with every bushel harvested on the frontier. Let us pray our walls can guard it."*

### 4. Governance & The Citadel Council

#### The Citadel Council of Lords
- **Technical Term**: DAO / Decentralized Governance Contract.
- **Lore Equivalent**: *The Citadel Council of Lords* or *The High Moot*.
- **Lore Context**: The parliamentary assembly where every deed-holder gathers to debate, sponsor, and vote on laws governing the kingdom. Voting power is strictly proportional to the number of parcels a lord governs.
- **In-Game Dialogue**:  
  > *"The High Jarl does not rule by whimsy; he must bow to the ratified decrees of the Citadel Council."*

#### The Four Royal Decree Types
The four categories of proposals codified in `ProposalType`:
1. **Decree of Royal Tithe** (`SetTaxRate`): Modifies the percentage of parcel harvest revenue diverted to the common kingdom treasury (standard 10%).
2. **Decree of Sovereign Dividend** (`DistributeTreasury`): Authorizes the disbursement of stored treasury gold directly to deed-holding lords based on land holdings.
3. **Declaration of Kingdom Crusade** (`DeclareWar`): Ratifies formal warfare against the rival kingdom, mobilizing the vanguard to storm enemy bastions and seize treasury wealth.
4. **Grant of Public Works** (`FundInfrastructure`): Allocates royal treasury gold to fund communal border walls, sentry towers, and bridgehead fortifications.

### 5. Sovereign Warfare & Plunder

#### The War of Ratings / The Grand Crusade
- **Technical Term**: Deterministic Smart Contract Combat (`resolveWar()`).
- **Lore Equivalent**: *The Sovereign Crusade* or *The Trial of Iron and Stone*.
- **Lore Context**: In accordance with the 612 Covenant, wars between Boreal and Solara are not chaotic brawls; they are grand, deterministic clashes of kingdom might. The attacking realm mobilizes its collective **Vanguard Siege Prowess** (sum of all parcel attack ratings) against the defending realm's **Bastion Fortification Rating** (sum of all parcel defense ratings).
- **In-Game Dialogue**:  
  > *"If our vanguard prowess exceeds their bastions, their gates shall splinter and their vaults shall be laid bare!"*

#### Sacking the Vaults / War Reparations
- **Technical Term**: Plunder Mechanism (Attacker seizes 50% of Defender's `treasuryGold`).
- **Lore Equivalent**: *Sacking the Vaults* or *Seizing the Spoils of War*.
- **Lore Context**: When a kingdom’s attack vanquishes an enemy's defense, the victorious lords breach the rival citadel and plunder half of their accumulated treasury gold, hauling wagon-trains of alchemical silver back to their home vaults.
- **In-Game Dialogue**:  
  > *"Fifty thousand silver pieces plundered from the southern vaults! Every northern lord drinks free wine tonight!"*

### 6. The Astral Mind & World Automata

#### The Astral Whisper / The Inner Daemon
- **Technical Term**: AI Large Language Model (OpenAI / Anthropic / Gemini / Ollama).
- **Lore Equivalent**: *The Astral Whisper* or *The Muse of the First Folk*.
- **Lore Context**: The mysterious spiritual resonance that grants living speech, memories, and sharp wit to the inhabitants of Aethelgard. When a traveler speaks to Aldric or Sir Gareth, the words are carried upon the ethereal wind and answered by the living consciousness of the world.
- **In-Game Dialogue**:  
  > *"The old soldier stared into the fire, listening to the whispered counsel of the unseen spirits before replying."*

#### The Sentry's Beat / The Wanderer's Route
- **Technical Term**: NPC Waypoint Roaming System.
- **Lore Equivalent**: *The Sentry's Beat* or *The Pilgrim's Circuit*.
- **Lore Context**: The established daily routines of peasants, guards, and merchants who walk the roads of Aethelgard, tending fields, scanning riverbanks, and trading goods at customary milestones.
