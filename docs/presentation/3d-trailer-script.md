## 📜 Scene-by-Scene Script Breakdown

### Act I: The Living Continent (`00:00 - 00:30`)
*Establishing the sheer scale of the 3D continuous open world running directly in Google Chrome / Brave at 60 FPS.*

#### `00:00 - 00:08` | Scene 1: The Frostbound Descent
- **Camera**: Cinematic FPV drone dive swooping down from the jagged, snow-capped granite peaks of the Boreal Taiga. The camera skims inches above the frosted pines, banking sharply over the rushing rapids of the Great Divide river.
- **Audio / SFX**: Sub-bass rumble. Distant mountain tempest wind howling. The crystalline rush of river water spraying off boulders. Lone melancholic cello chord strikes.
- **On-Screen Text**:  
  ```
  [ ZERO DOWNLOADS. ZERO INSTALLS. 100% IN YOUR BROWSER. ]
  ```
- **Voiceover (VO)** *(Deep, gritty, resonant narrator)*:  
  *"For ten centuries, the Great Divide kept the peace. Frostfang in the North. Solara in the South. But true sovereignty is never handed down..."*

#### `00:08 - 00:18` | Scene 2: The Spawn & Third-Person Camera Snap
- **Camera**: The camera plunges toward an ancient cobblestone crossroads at coordinates `(X: 0, Z: -25)`. A hooded wanderer clad in iron plate stands beneath a rustic lantern post. The camera seamlessly whips around behind his right shoulder into an authentic **GTA-style over-the-shoulder third-person camera**.
- **Player Input**: Player presses `[W]`, followed by `[Shift]`.
- **Visual**: Wanderer accelerates into a full sprint. Camera slightly pitches and pulls back dynamically with spring-arm physics, capturing the cloth physics of the adventurer's slate-blue cloak whipping in the breeze.
- **HUD**: Subtle minimalist circular radar mini-map rotates smoothly in the bottom-left corner. Compass tape at top-center swings as the player steers across the cobblestones toward the river crossing.
- **Audio / SFX**: Heavy boot-falls striking cobblestones with reverb. Iron chainmail clinking. War drums begin a rhythmic, driving 110 BPM march tempo.

#### `00:18 - 00:30` | Scene 3: The Crossroads of Civilizations
- **Camera**: Tracking low-angle side tracking shot, orbiting 45 degrees as the player vaults over a weathered timber fence (`[Space]` Jump impulse).
- **Visual**: In the distance across the water, the gleaming marble towers and royal crimson banners of Solara Keep reflect the afternoon sun, contrasting with the dark basalt battlements of Frostfang Citadel behind the player.
- **VO**:  
  *"...it is carved into the earth. One meter at a time."*

### Act II: Sovereignty & In-World Construction (`00:30 - 01:00`)
*Demonstrating true cryptographic spatial ownership and frictionless real-time building.*

#### `00:30 - 00:40` | Scene 4: Claiming the Frontier
- **Camera**: Over-the-shoulder camera decelerates as the player approaches a pulsing cyan boundary pillar anchored into the bedrock at Parcel `#042`.
- **HUD / UI**: An interactive holographic prompt glows in 3D world-space above the pillar:  
  `[B] CLAIM PARCEL DEED • 0.01 AMOY`
- **Player Input**: Player taps `[B]`.
- **Visual**: A radiant shockwave of golden light pulses outward from the pillar along the ground, tracing a sharp 30m x 30m perimeter box. A sleek Web3 confirmation toast slides smoothly from the top-right:  
  `✓ Transaction Confirmed: Parcel #042 Minted to 0x71C...49b`.
- **Audio / SFX**: Deep resonant chime with metallic ring. Electric hum of activating magical runes.
- **VO**:  
  *"No central server owns this turf. Your parcel is an ERC-721 deed recorded directly on Polygon Amoy. What you own... is truly yours."*

#### `00:40 - 00:52` | Scene 5: Holographic Construction Mode
- **Player Input**: Player taps `[C]`.
- **Visual**: The HUD transitions into Construction Mode. A radial building wheel appears momentarily, selecting the *Tier 1 Farmstead*. A glowing wireframe hologram of a medieval timber-and-stone farmhouse hovers in front of the player, snapping dynamically to the terrain elevation.
- **Camera**: Slow rotating semi-overhead crane shot.
- **Player Input**: Player taps `[R]` to rotate 90 degrees, positioning the door facing the river road, then clicks the left mouse button `[LMB]` to place.
- **Visual**: The wireframe flashes neon emerald green. Beams of raw timber and solid stone blocks materialize from the earth with a burst of dust particles, settling into a fully rendered, physics-colliding building. A small waterwheel begins spinning beside a freshly dug irrigation ditch.
- **Audio / SFX**: Heavy thud of timber locking into granite. Sound of rushing water engaging the wheel. Mechanical click of UI confirmation.

#### `00:52 - 01:00` | Scene 6: The Gold Harvest
- **Visual**: Floating above the farmhouse chimney, a shimmering golden coin counter begins ticking upward: `+2.5 GOLD / HR`. The player approaches the door, taps `[E] Harvest`, and a burst of gold dust showers into their top resource bar (`Gold: 1,420`).
- **VO**:  
  *"Build your homestead. Erect guard towers. Levy kingdom tithes. Every stone you place shifts the wealth of the realm."*

### Act III: Living Minds of Iron (`01:00 - 01:30`)
*Introducing Bring-Your-Own-AI (BYOAI) autonomous NPCs roaming the world with real LLM reasoning.*

#### `01:00 - 01:10` | Scene 7: The Patrol on the Bridge
- **Camera**: Low-angle shot looking up the cobblestone slope toward the great arched bridge of the Great Divide. A towering knight in full fluted plate armor with a direwolf-crested shield—**Sir Gareth**—paces his patrol route.
- **Visual**: As the player enters within 5 meters, Sir Gareth halts mid-stride, turning smoothly on his heel to face the player directly. A stylized 3D billboard speech bubble pops above his steel visor:  
  `"Halt, traveler! The Northern border is sealed by decree of High Jarl Vance."`
- **Audio / SFX**: Heavy steel greaves clicking on stone. Unsheathing sound of a bastard sword resting against an iron targe.
- **HUD**: In-world prompt: `[E] Speak with Sir Gareth`.

  `"I hold the deed to Parcel #042. Solara merchants are mobilizing downriver. What are your orders?"`
