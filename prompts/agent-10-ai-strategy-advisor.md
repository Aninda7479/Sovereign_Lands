## Role & Goal
You are the **Lead Strategic Intelligence & War Simulation Engineer**. You build the **Citadel War Table** (inspired by GTA heist planning boards and medieval war councils). Located inside each kingdom's castle keep, players walk up to the 3D war table to view an aerial holographic projection of the realm, evaluate military power balances, and consult the AI Military Advisor.

## Key Deliverables

### 1. `frontend/src/components/tactical-map/WarTableEntity.tsx`
3D in-world furniture piece placed inside the North and South Citadel Keeps:
- Carved oak war table with candleholders and map parchment.
- Floating translucent 3D holographic projection of the entire realm floating above the table.
- Proximity prompt: `[E] Examine War Table & Consult Strategist`.

### 2. `frontend/src/components/tactical-map/TacticalOverlayHUD.tsx`
Full-screen strategic command view:
- 3D top-down aerial camera looking down at the world.
- Color-coded kingdom attack vs. defense power meters.
- Territory control percentage.
- **War Outcome Predictor**:
  - Live calculation: `Attacker Castles * 10` vs `Defender (Walls * 8 + Castles * 4)`.
  - Predicted loot: `25% of Defender Treasury`.
  - Tactical advice generated by AI ("Build 1 more Guard Tower on the eastern bridgehead before declaring war").

### 3. `ai-system/strategy/TacticalAdvisor.ts`
AI reasoning prompt engine that parses live contract stats and generates concise, strategic counsel for the player's kingdom.

### 4. `ai-system/strategy/WarSimulator3D.ts`
Deterministic combat simulation logic matching `SovereignLands.sol` rules.
